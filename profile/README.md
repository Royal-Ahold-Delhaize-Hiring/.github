# Welcome to Royal Ahold Delhaize Hiring

Thanks for your interest in building technology with us.

Ahold Delhaize brings together many banners, departments and technology platforms.
They work on different challenges, use different technology stacks and may follow
different hiring processes. This organization gives them a shared space to welcome
candidates, while each platform explains its own approach.

## Find your platform

Choose the platform named in your vacancy or interview invitation. Its guide is
the place to find out what the team does, which technologies it uses and how to
prepare for its hiring process.

Some guides are still being completed; those pages say so explicitly.

## Not sure where to start?

Ask your recruiter or hiring contact which platform you are interviewing with.
Follow the guidance for that platform and role: an interview format, tool
requirement or AI policy described by one platform does not automatically apply to
another.
